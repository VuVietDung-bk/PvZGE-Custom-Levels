Pack your plants and come up with a plan to stand up against these hungry zombies in this BIG (?) Sandbox Level Pack! Carefully (I did my best) crafted and balanced levels that are both fun and challenging featuring:

- One sandbox level for each world that will be updated as more content are added (if I'm not busy lol) with various attack patterns!   E.g. Far Future starts out more slowly but will ramp up quickly while Big Wave Beach will rapidly rush you down.
- Different difficulties! Yes you heard it right, ranging from D (easiest, less dangerous zombies) to A (hardest, features ALL zombies   in that world). You can change these difficulties in settings.
- Dynamic zombie spawn combined with set, predictable ambush events.
- Lots of gimmicks from lots of world! You ever wanna have both Sun Bombs and Sunflowers? You got it!
- Challenge yourself in the ultimate sandbox level Modern Day, featuring every single (most) zombie from every single world!

Feedback is much appreciated, I will try to update these levels as best I can. Have fun!
